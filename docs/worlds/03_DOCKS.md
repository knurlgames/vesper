# World 3: The Docks (brief, proposal)

The harbour, at night, in a storm. It leans covert, and it's the most vertical world so far.
- **Host:** Silke (snow leopard). **Gear:** the rope. **Vesper's lesson:** the ceiling crawl and the headfirst
  descent.
- **The link in the chain:** the parts inside the toys come in by sea, from abroad, labelled "haberdashery".
- **Target length:** 70–90 minutes on a first visit.

## The story
- **Coming in:** Mrs. Gander's shipping notes name a ship, the *SS Prudence*, due in on tonight's tide. Silke has
  been watching the harbour for a week already. She doesn't say so; there's a sleeping bag on the crane.
- **The Docks:** warehouses, cranes, rain, a fish market opening at four in the morning. Swyne's face is on a
  hoarding over the harbour: **A DECENT PORT FOR A DECENT CITY**.
- **Going out:** the crates hold thousands of small brass coils marked with a spiral, the receivers. The ship leaves
  without its cargo. The harbour master's charts show where the coils go next: a wellness retreat in the hills.
- **Vesper** watches from the top of the lighthouse.

## The map
A harbour with two quays, a breakwater and a lighthouse, and the *Prudence* in the middle of it.

| Area | What it is | Mostly |
|---|---|---|
| **The Quay** | warehouses, stacked containers, cranes, rail lines | covert |
| **The Customs House** | an old stone office, a long counter, a records room, a bonded store | blend in |
| **The Fish Market** | an open hall that fills before dawn | blend in |
| **The SS Prudence** | a cargo ship: the deck, two holds, the engine room, the bridge, the mast | both |
| **The Breakwater and lighthouse** | a long stone arm out into the storm | climbing |
| **The Dry Dock** | an emptied dock with a hull up on blocks | the boss |

- **Wardrobes:** Silke's crane cab, a locker in the Customs House, and the ship's laundry.
- **Shortcuts:** a crane's hook from the Quay to the ship's deck, and a pipe from the engine room out to the
  breakwater.

## How it teaches
1. **Vesper's lesson:** under the Quay's wharf, she hangs upside down from a beam by her feet, talking. Then she comes
   down a warehouse wall head first. Nim learns both: crawl along beams and pipes from beneath, and descend any
   climbable wall head first, which is faster and lands in a pounce.
2. **Silke's rope** after her test (Key 1): swing from rings and crane hooks, pull crates down, bind from a distance.
3. **The suit at the docks:** the Customs House and the fish market, posing as an inspector or a buyer.

## Enemies
- **Goons:** dock rats in oilskins. They fight, and they work the cranes.
- **Searchlights** on the cranes and the ship, swept by a rat in each cab. Out of the light, she's unseen.
- **Gulls** again, and now cormorants on the pilings, which dive at her if she's spotted.
- **Customs officers:** stoats in uniform. Swyne pays them. They won't fight, but they blow whistles.
- **People in trances:** five dockers who opened a crate, stacking boxes in neat pyramids in the rain.

## The set piece: the storm
The *Prudence* casts off early to get away with the cargo.
- Nim crosses from the Quay to the moving ship by rope, swinging from crane hook to crane hook as they swing in the
  wind.
- On board, she climbs the mast and swings down rigging lines to cut each crate's net with a pounce, dropping the
  cargo into the harbour.
- Searchlights sweep the deck. She hangs under the rails to stay out of them.
- The tide drags the ship back. She drops anchor herself with a rope pull.
- **Punchline:** the crates float. They all wash up on the Fish Market's slipway by morning, and the market sells
  them as "lace".

## The boss: Harbourmaster Mortimer Tusk
A walrus in an admiral's coat he wasn't issued. He fights in the Dry Dock, round a hull up on blocks.
1. **The crane.** He swings containers at her from a crane cab. Nim ropes onto each container as it passes and rides
   it to the cab.
2. **The flood.** He opens the dock gates, and the dock floods fast. Nim crawls along pipes under the gantries, out of
   the water, to reach the gate controls.
3. **The hull.** He makes his stand on the hull's keel. She comes down the hull head first and pounces him.
- **Punchline:** he's bound to the ship's own anchor, and the anchor is lowered, slowly, to a sensible depth for him
  to stand in.

## What Nim leaves with
- The rope. The ceiling crawl and the headfirst descent.
- The charts, which point to a wellness retreat in the hills, and the first look at the receivers.
- Silke's verdict: nothing. She leaves her crane cab's key in Nim's hand.
