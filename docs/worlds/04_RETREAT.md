# World 4: The Retreat (brief, proposal)

A wellness retreat in the hills, where the hypnosis is made and tested. It's personal: Nim was held here.
- **Host:** Lull (serval). **Gear:** the pendant. **Vesper's lesson:** Hold the Room, the last thing she teaches.
- **The link in the chain:** the receivers are tested here. Everyone under waits for one signal.
- **Target length:** 70–90 minutes on a first visit.

## The story
- **Coming in:** Tusk's charts point to **Serenity Springs**, a retreat in the hills. Nim knows the name before she
  knows why. Lull has been there as a guest for a week, offering "sessions". "They think I'm one of them. I've never
  been so insulted."
- **Serenity Springs:** terraces of hot springs, white robes, a gong every hour. A plaque over the door: **OPENED BY
  COUNCILLOR SWYNE, FOR THE DECENT RECOVERY OF THE PURRLANDS**.
- **The middle:** in the old sanatorium wing, Nim finds her own patient file. Subject N, with a photograph, and the
  note **"Not suggestible. Discard."** That's why the toys don't work on her.
- **Going out:** the doctor's notes show the receivers are waiting for a single broadcast. Where and when, they don't
  say. The order forms are from the Decency Press.
- **Vesper** watches from the bell tower, and this is the world where she steps in. See "Vesper, once".

## The map
A hillside retreat, its terraces going up to an old sanatorium at the top.

| **The Springs** | terraces of hot pools, steam, wooden walkways, a waterfall | both |
| **The Treatment Wing** | white corridors, therapy rooms behind frosted glass, the "listening" rooms | both |

- **Wardrobes:** Lull's guest room, a linen room in the Treatment Wing, and a boathouse by the lower pool.
- **Shortcuts:** the waterfall's back ledge from the Springs to the Gardens, and a laundry chute from the Sanatorium
  down to Reception.

## How it teaches
1. **Lull's pendant** after her test (Key 1): it wakes people at range, and lets Nim fake a trance. While she's
   "under", goons ignore her as long as she walks slowly with her eyes half shut. Running, climbing or a held button
   breaks it.
2. **Vesper's lesson, Hold the Room:** in the Lounge, Vesper lets her own cover blow on purpose. Twelve guards turn.
   She looks at them. They stop, one by one, and go back to their posts. Then Nim learns it: when her cover's blown, a
   stare-down (hold, then release on the beat) bluffs the whole room back to calm.

## Enemies
- **Orderlies:** sheep in white tunics. Docile, until they're not. They fight, and they can be bound.
- **The quiet staff:** **herons** in white coats who stand perfectly still in the pools and see everything. They don't fight; they ring the gong.
- **The gong** brings every orderly in the area.
- **People in trances:** five guests who stayed too long, doing the same stretch by the pool, forever. Nobody hurts
  them.

## The set piece: the session
Dr. Strix holds a group session in the Treatment Wing's great room. Nim joins the group in a fake trance.
- The doctor gives commands to the room: stand, turn, kneel, walk to the window. Nim must obey each in time, or
  she's spotted.
- Between commands, while the doctor turns away, Nim acts: lifts a key, swaps a tape, pockets the session notes.
- The commands come faster. The pendant wakes the others one by one, quietly, and they keep pretending too.
- **Punchline:** at the end, the doctor says "Wake." The whole room opens its eyes at once, looks at her, and walks
  out.

## Vesper, once
The one time she shows her full strength. After the file, the doctor's whole staff corners Nim in the Sanatorium,
more than she can fight. Vesper walks in through the front door, alone, unhurried. She says nothing. Every orderly in
the room kneels. Then she helps Nim up.
"That's what the Kneel is for. Eventually."

## The boss: Dr. Strix
An owl, the retreat's founder. Her eyes do the hypnosis, and they don't work on Nim. She fights in the Sensory Hall.
1. **The spirals.** She turns every guest in the hall into a shield. Nim wakes them with the pendant, one at a time,
   while the doctor moves among them.
2. **The lights.** She flies between the hanging lights. Nim climbs and swings between them on the rope, and pounces
   her down.
3. **The stare.** On the floor, the doctor stares at her, everything she has. Nim holds the room. The doctor blinks
   first.
- **Punchline:** bound in her own treatment chair, with her own tape on a loop: "Decent and calm and sleepy."

## What Nim leaves with
- The pendant, Hold the Room, and Vesper's last lesson. From here, Vesper teaches nothing new.
- Her file, and the reason the toys never worked on her.
- The order forms from the Decency Press.
- Lull's verdict: "You're not suggestible. I'm going to have to try much harder with you." Nim: "Please don't."
