# World 5: The Decency Press (brief, proposal)

The factory. It leans covert, and it's the first world where Vesper isn't watching.
- **Host:** Rivet (caracal). **Gear:** lures. **No new lesson:** Vesper has taught everything. This world tests it.
- **The link in the chain:** the Coalition's pamphlet printers out front, the toy factory behind.
- **Target length:** 70–90 minutes on a first visit.

## The story
- **Coming in:** the Retreat's order forms come from the Decency Press, the Coalition's printers in the industrial
  quarter. Rivet has a schematic of the building already. She drew it from memory after a tour. "They gave me a
  pamphlet. I gave them nothing."
- **The same night:** Vesper is called before the Council's Decency Committee. She goes, in her coat, calm. Nim hears
  it on the radio as she works: Swyne names "a certain club in the Old Quarter" in public, and Vesper doesn't deny
  it. The bell tower in this world is empty.
- **The Press:** a red-brick works with a chimney. Out front, a reading room full of pamphlets: **DECENCY: A GUIDE
  FOR THE FAMILY**. Behind it, the line, making toys.
- **Going out:** the foreman's office holds the broadcast schedule: midnight, City Hall's clock tower, the night of
  the Decency Act vote. And an order for six hundred gift bags for the Council's Decency Ball.

## The map
A factory in four halls, with a yard and a chimney.

| Area | What it is | Mostly |
|---|---|---|
| **The Reading Room** | the shopfront: pamphlets, a counter, a Coalition volunteer | blend in |
| **The Print Floor** | presses, paper rolls, drying racks overhead | covert |
| **The Line** | the toy assembly line: conveyors, stamps, a paint shop, a testing booth | covert, the set piece |
| **The Loading Yard** | vans, a weighbridge, a gatehouse with dogs | covert |
| **The Boiler House and chimney** | the furnace and a 60 m chimney with a ladder | climbing |
| **The Foundry** | the furnace's floor, under the chimney | the boss |

- **Wardrobes:** Rivet's van in the yard, the Reading Room's cloakroom, and the boiler house's staff room.
- **Shortcuts:** a paper chute from the Print Floor down to the Yard, and a gantry from the Line across to the Boiler
  House.

## How it teaches
Nothing new from Vesper. Rivet's **lures** after her test (Key 1): throw a seized toy, it buzzes, and guards go to
look. A world built to use everything together, with the lures as the extra.

## Enemies
- **Goons:** moles in overalls and goggles, who work the line and fight. They're poor-sighted, so lures work twice as
  well on them.
- **Guard dogs** in the Yard: Dobermans on chains, with handlers. They smell her. Lures, or the roofs.
- **Quality control:** a magpie on a high stool over the line, who watches for anything out of place. Doesn't fight,
  pulls the line's alarm.
- **People in trances:** five testers who tried the products, still sitting in the testing booth. Nobody hurts them.

## The set piece: the line, backwards
Rivet's plan: run the whole line in reverse, so it unmakes what it made.
- Nim fights through the Line to the control room and throws the switch.
- The conveyors run backwards and pick up speed. She rides them upstream, through the stamps, the paint shop, the
  testing booth, jumping between belts as presses come down in reverse.
- The magpie pulls the alarm. Goons come down the belts toward her. She lures them off the sides.
- At the end of the line, everything that's been made goes back in as raw parts.
- **Punchline:** the line's last trick, run backwards, is to reprint the Reading Room's pamphlets as blank paper.

## The boss: Foreman Grubb
A mole in a giant press of his own design, the **Decency Engine**: a printing press on legs. He fights in the Foundry.
1. **The plates.** The Engine stamps the floor in patterns. Nim reads the pattern, and the lures make him stamp the
   wrong way.
2. **The ink.** He floods the floor with ink, and she can't stay on it. She climbs the walls and swings across the
   ceiling chains with the rope.
3. **The feed.** She drops down into the Engine's feed and, from inside, the crop jams the rollers.
- **Punchline:** the Engine, jammed, prints its last sheet: Grubb's signed confession, which he'd written in case
  anyone asked. Bound to the press. Nobody's printing anything tonight.

## What Nim leaves with
- The lures, and the knowledge she can do it alone.
- The broadcast schedule: midnight, City Hall's clock tower, the night of the vote. And the gift bags for the Ball.
- Back at the club, Vesper's home from the hearing. She doesn't talk about it. She asks how the chimney was.
