# World 2: Primrose Close (brief, proposal)

The suburbs. It leans blend-in, and it's where the hypocrisy is thickest.
- **Host:** Grizelda (Pallas's cat). **Gear:** the Scold. **Vesper's lesson:** the commands Fetch and Open.
- **The link in the chain:** Swyne's toys are sold at home "demonstration parties", hosted by the Decency League's
  own ladies and aimed at councillors' homes.
- **Target length:** 60–80 minutes on a first visit.

## The story
- **Coming in:** the Pier's ledger lists 40 party orders on one street. Grizelda lived there once. "Twelve years. I
  still dream about the hedges."
- **The Close:** identical houses, identical lawns, net curtains, a church hall with a bake sale for the Decency
  League. Every house has a SWYNE FOR DECENCY sign on its lawn, and a party booked for tonight.
- **Going out:** the top saleswoman's order book shows the parties are booked into councillors' homes, on purpose.
  Next morning's parish newsletter thanks Mrs. Gander for "a record year".
- **Vesper** sits on the church's steeple, watching.

## The map
A cul-de-sac of sixteen houses, with back gardens, a church and allotments behind.

- **Wardrobes:** Grizelda's old house (number 4, for sale), the vicarage's laundry, and a garden shed.
- **Shortcuts:** a gap in the hedge from the allotments into the Close, and the church's bell rope down from the
  steeple into the hall.

## How it teaches
1. **Vesper's lesson, in the suit:** at the Neighbourhood Watch hut she asks a meerkat for the hut's key. He fetches
   it. She asks him to open the shed. He does. Then it's Nim's turn: Fetch and Open, and harder asks cost Composure.
2. **The Back Gardens, in garb:** fences as tightropes, washing lines, greenhouse roofs that crack under a heavy
   landing.
3. **Grizelda's Scold** after her test (Key 1). It freezes every goon nearby, and the whole street's net curtains
   twitch.

## Enemies
- **Goons:** bulldog delivery men in brown overalls, carrying boxes marked DECENCY LEAGUE: LITERATURE.
- **Neighbourhood Watch:** meerkats on lookout posts and porches. They don't fight; they ring the street's phone tree.
  Command them, or stay off their lawns.
- **Curtain twitchers:** every window with a lit net curtain is a pair of eyes. Garb gets spotted, the suit doesn't.
- **People in trances:** five neighbours who bought early, mowing the same strip of lawn over and over. Nobody hurts
  them.

## The set piece: the demonstration party
The saleswoman's consultant for tonight is held up. Nim takes her place, in the suit.
- She hosts a living room of League ladies. Each round she presents a toy from the kit and answers a question
  (dialogue choices), while swapping each hypnotic toy for a dud from her bag when nobody's looking.
- Composure is the meter. A wrong answer costs it, a good one earns it back, and a swap seen costs a lot.
- Fetch and Open get the ladies' husbands out of the room and the kit cupboard open.
- **Punchline:** the ladies love the honest advice the duds come with. Nim's booked for three more parties.

## The boss: Mrs. Marjorie Gander
A goose, the League's top saleswoman and Swyne's most loyal supporter in public. In private, his best customer.
She fights in her conservatory and topiary garden.
1. **The garden party.** Her guests are in trances and wander the lawns. Nim mustn't hurt them, and Gander hides among
   them throwing teacups. The pendant isn't hers yet, so she tracks Gander by the honking.
2. **The sprinklers.** Gander floods the garden from her control box. Nim climbs the topiary out of the water, and the
   crop knocks out each sprinkler head.
3. **The tantrum.** Cornered in the conservatory, Gander throws everything she owns. You don't fight a tantrum. Nim
   uses Grizelda's Scold, and Gander freezes, mid-throw, then sits down on her own.
- **Punchline:** Nim binds her to her own garden bench under a sign she made herself: SWYNE FOR DECENCY.

## What Nim leaves with
- The Scold. The commands Fetch and Open.
- The order book, which points to the parties in councillors' houses, and, under it, the shipping notes for the Docks.
- Grizelda's verdict: "Adequate. Don't let it go to your head." Then, to Vesper, privately: "She's good."

## Owner review
- Every joke in the Key table, the names Marjorie Gander and St. Prudence's, and Vesper's and Grizelda's lines.
- The demonstration party: it's a toy party in a living room, and it stays suggestive (product names, boxes, the
  ladies' reactions). Nothing is shown in use.
