# The Finale: City Hall (brief, proposal)

Midnight, the night of the vote. Vesper's last night. Owner-decided: City Hall, and she dies on her own terms.
- **No host, no gear.** Everything Nim has, used together.
- **The door:** about 58 Keys (her rank in the order), opened from the raided club.
- **Target length:** 40–60 minutes.

## Before it: the final exam
The raided club. The door's sealed with police tape, the dungeon's dark, the crew's scattered. Vesper is the only one
there, waiting for Nim.
- She sets her the exam she set every sister. A course through the empty club, one room per lesson. The takedown
  from above, the commands, the ceiling, Hold the Room, and one room each for every sister's gear. The Rope's own
  version of a recap quiz, played, not answered.
- At the end, Vesper gives no verdict. She takes the tape off the door. "Shall we?"

## The story
- **City Hall:** the Council chamber, packed. The vote is at midnight, and the clock tower's broadcast will chime at
  the same time. Every councillor still carrying a receiver will vote yes. Swyne swapped some of them back.
- **The crew** is out on the city: waking people who've gone under early, holding the chamber's doors, getting the
  receivers out of councillors' pockets. Nim and Vesper go up.
- **Vesper draws her whip** for the first time in the game, at the foot of the tower. Her goons don't get up.
- **The tower:** the broadcast is in the clock, and it's fed by the bells. It can't be switched off once it's running.
  It can be stopped from inside the mechanism, by hand, by someone who stays there.

## The chamber
- Nim walks in during the debate in the suit. The signet lets her command the chamber's officers.
- Councillors on the floor are going under as midnight nears. The pendant wakes them, one at a time, while she stays
  in character.
- Swyne sees her from the dais. He makes a speech about her: "There. That is what decency is up against." Her cover
  is blown in front of the whole Council.

## The boss: Councillor Swyne
1. **The chamber.** He turns the room on her. Councillors in trances come at her, and nobody hurts them. She wakes
   them with the pendant while his footmen fight her.
2. **The tower.** He runs up the tower as midnight nears. Nim climbs after him, up the outside: the ivy, the
   ornaments, the dials. Every quarter-chime is a pulse that knocks her loose if she's not holding on.
3. **The clock face.** He makes his stand on the minute hand as it climbs to twelve. Everything she has: the crop,
   the rope, the lures, the Scold. She binds him on the hand itself.
- **Punchline:** none. He's still talking as she lowers him to the police.

## Vesper
The broadcast starts anyway: it was set to run from the bells, not by Swyne.
- Vesper looks at the mechanism, and at Nim. She's already decided.
- She sends Nim down with Swyne. Nim refuses. Vesper: **"I'm not asking. For once, I'm telling you."**
- Nim goes. Vesper climbs into the clockwork.
- From the street, Nim and the crew watch midnight not strike. The broadcast stops. The clock stops at a minute to
  twelve.
- In the chamber, the councillors wake. The vote fails.

## After
- **The club:** the crew reopens the Velvet Rope. Nobody sits in Vesper's chair. Augusta gives Nim Vesper's coat.
- **The hub's final state:** the club is lit again, and the clock on City Hall still reads a minute to twelve.
  Nobody's fixed it. Nobody will.
- **Post-credits (proposal, owner's call):** a short coda in an afterlife's waiting room, Vesper taking a ticket.
  It's the only link to Nine Lives, and it's made from scratch: no Nine Lives assets.
