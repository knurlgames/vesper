# World 6: The Decency Ball (brief, proposal)

The Council's grand hall, the night of the Ball. It leans blend-in, and the whole crew works it at once.
- **Host:** Augusta (lioness). **Gear:** the signet. **No new lesson.**
- **The link in the chain:** Swyne in public, giving every councillor a toy as a party favour.
- **Target length:** 70–90 minutes on a first visit.

## The story
- **Coming in:** six hundred gift bags, one for every guest at the Council's Decency Ball, each holding a receiver.
  The vote is next week. Augusta's been invited to the Ball for forty years and has never gone. "It seemed a shame to
  waste the gown."
- **The plan:** the crew swaps every gift bag for a harmless one while Nim keeps the room's attention.
- **The Ball:** chandeliers, an orchestra, a glass dome, councillors and their wives. Swyne gives the welcome speech
  on decency, then works the room handing out the bags himself.
- **Vesper is a guest.** She comes in on Augusta's invitation and dances with Swyne. In public. In front of everyone.
  He knows who she is now. She lets him.
- **Going out:** the bags are swapped, but Swyne's secretary saw Nim's face. Before dawn, the police raid the Velvet
  Claw. The club is emptied, its door sealed. The crew scatters to safehouses. The vote is moved to tomorrow night.

  the floor.

## How it teaches
Nothing new from Vesper. Augusta's **signet** after her test (Key 1): Commands now work on the powerful.
Councillors, their aides, the Ball's master of ceremonies. Without it, they ignore her.

## Enemies
- **Goons:** goats in footmen's livery, the Council's own staff. They fight once her cover's blown.
- **Private security:** a Rottweiler at every door to the Galleries. Commanded with the signet, or avoided.
- **Swyne's secretary,** watching the room the whole night (the boss).
- **The guests:** the powerful, as a crowd. They're the cover.
- **People in trances:** five guests who opened a bag early, dancing the same waltz with no partner. Nobody hurts
  them.

## The set piece: the swap
The crew swaps six hundred gift bags while Nim holds the room. The whole crew is on screen, each at her post.
- The Ballroom's attention is a shared meter. Nim keeps it on her: a dance, a word with a councillor, the wrong card
  at the speech. Commands with the signet steer guests away from the cloakroom.
- Every minute, cut to the crew: Silke lowers bags down through the dome, Rivet sorts in the cloakroom, Dash runs them
  through the kitchens, Lull wakes anyone who's already opened one, Grizelda keeps the waiters in line. Bastion holds
  the door.
- If the room's attention slips, the secretary heads for the cloakroom. Nim heads him off.
- **Punchline:** the new gift bags each hold one of the Coalition's own pamphlets, blank, from the Press.

## The boss: Mr. Horace Tupping (name approved by the owner, 2026-11-11)
A ram, Swyne's private secretary. He sees Nim for what she is halfway through the night.
1. **The waltz.** He cuts in. A duel in public, on the dance floor: he leads, she follows, and each of his steps is a
   question. Answer in step, or lose Composure. The floor watches.
2. **The galleries.** Her cover's blown. It's a fight now, along the galleries, footmen coming from both ends. The
   signet clears a councillor's guard out of the way; the rope gets her across.
3. **The chandelier.** He takes the chandelier's chain up toward the dome to get out. She rides the chandelier up
   after him, swinging it to reach him.
- **Punchline:** bound to the chandelier, lowered to just above the dance floor, he turns slowly to the music for the
  rest of the Ball.

## What Nim leaves with
- The signet, and the gift bags swapped.
- Tupping saw her face. The raid comes before dawn.
- Augusta's verdict: "You dance like a soldier. It'll do." Then, to Vesper: "It's time."
